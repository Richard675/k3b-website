How bulk orders work

One list, one quote

Bulk prices depend on the products, quantities and timing of each order, so every wholesale order is quoted individually.

  1. Share your requirements

    Products or looks you need, quantities in boxes, pieces or square metres, and your project location.

  2. We check and quote

    We confirm availability and send prices for your list.

  3. Agree and arrange

    Once you accept, we arrange collection or delivery with you directly.
